Clean in four steps

A full wipe takes about a minute.

  1. Pick a durationChoose 15s, 30s, 1m, 2m or ∞ (no time limit) at the bottom of the start screen.
  2. Lock the keyboardClick Lock & Clean or press ⌘↩. KeysCleaner fills the screen and stops every key press from reaching your Mac.
  3. Wipe awayClean the keys however you like. Each key turns clean on the screen the moment you press it, and the counter shows how many are done.
  4. UnlockThe keyboard unlocks by itself when the timer runs out. To finish early, press and hold Hold to unlock for about a second. You'll then see a short report with any keys you missed.

Frequently asked questions

Click a question to see the answer.

Does KeysCleaner need any special permissions?

No. KeysCleaner doesn't ask for Accessibility access or install anything. It blocks keys while its own fullscreen cleaning screen is in front, which is why it takes over the whole screen during cleaning.

How do I stop cleaning before the timer ends?

Press and hold the Hold to unlock button with the mouse or trackpad for about a second. Letting go early cancels it, so a quick swipe or tap while you clean the trackpad won't unlock the keyboard.

Can I add more time while cleaning?

Yes. Click + 30s next to the unlock button as many times as you need. If you'd rather not watch the clock at all, choose ∞ and the keyboard stays locked until you hold to unlock.

Why are the Dock and menu bar hidden while cleaning?

So nothing can pull you out of the cleaning screen by accident. While the keyboard is locked, the Dock, the menu bar and app switching (⌘⇥) are unavailable, and any extra displays are covered. Everything comes back the moment you unlock.

Why don't some function keys light up?

macOS handles brightness, volume and other media keys itself, so those presses may never reach KeysCleaner. That's why Esc, the function row and fn are shown as a bonus and aren't part of the 63 keys you need for a full clean.

Does it work with external keyboards?

Yes. Every keyboard connected to your Mac is locked during cleaning. The on-screen map shows a MacBook layout, so extra keys such as a numeric keypad are blocked but not shown.

The key labels don't match my keyboard language.

The map follows the physical position of each key, so it works the same whatever input language you use. The labels follow the US layout.

How do I test my keys after cleaning?

Just press keys on the start screen. Each key lights up on the map while you hold it, and nothing is typed anywhere.

Does KeysCleaner record what I press?

No. Key presses are only used to block them and to light up keys on the screen. They're never stored or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 15.1 or later, on any Mac.
