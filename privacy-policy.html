1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KeysCleaner (“the app”, “we”, “us”), a macOS app that temporarily locks your keyboard so you can clean it, handles information. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it never sends anything off your Mac.

2. Keyboard input

To lock your keyboard, the app receives the key presses that are sent to it while it is in front. It uses them for two things only:

  • to stop them from reaching your Mac while the keyboard is locked, and
  • to light up the matching key on the on-screen keyboard and count how many keys you have wiped.

Key presses are handled in memory and are never recorded, stored or transmitted. The app doesn't see what you type in other apps and doesn't keep any history of keys. The cleaning report shows only which keys were pressed during that session, and it's discarded when you close it.

3. Information stored on your Mac

To remember your preferences, the app keeps a few settings in its own local storage on your Mac:

  • the cleaning duration you last chose,
  • the date of your last cleaning, and
  • how many times you have cleaned.

These settings never leave your Mac and are removed when you delete the app.

4. Network, analytics and third parties

KeysCleaner makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party code, and it does not use cookies or any form of tracking.

5. Permissions

The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and doesn't ask for Accessibility access or access to your files, contacts, location, camera or microphone. While cleaning, it temporarily hides the Dock and menu bar and covers extra displays. Everything is restored when you unlock.

6. Children's privacy

KeysCleaner doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
